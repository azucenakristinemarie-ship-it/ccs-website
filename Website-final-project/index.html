About CCS

Overview

The College of Computer Studies (CCS) is committed to developing competent, innovative, and ethically responsible computing professionals equipped with the knowledge, technical skills, and values needed to thrive in the rapidly evolving digital world. Through quality instruction, experiential learning, research, and community engagement, the College prepares students to become globally competitive graduates capable of addressing real-world challenges through technology.

Guided by its commitment to academic excellence and innovation, the College continuously enhances its curriculum, learning environment, and industry partnerships to ensure that graduates are well-prepared to become responsible technology professionals, entrepreneurs, researchers, and future leaders in the field of computing.

De La Salle John Bosco College

VISION and MISSION

Vision

De La Salle John Bosco is a leading Lasallian educational institution inspired by the life and teachings of Jesus Christ in providing a nurturing learning environment for the holistic formation of the members of the community and partners.

Mission

Animated by the Lasallian core values, De La Salle John Bosco College promotes the advancement of academic, research and technological excellence, culture of innovation, and vocation and sustainable community engagement.

College of Computer Studies

Departmental Objectives

In keeping with the vision-mission-goals of the institution, the College is committed to:

1

Provide relevant and responsive academic programs imbued with the skills, attitudes and values needed to develop qualified, highly trained and professionalized Filipino Lasallian graduates dedicated to the service of others.

2

Instill appreciation of the Lasallian values of self-actualization and the continuous quest for the fulfillment of each student’s dreams and vision.

3

Dedicate the student’s Lasallian education to the transformation of a just and humane environment, enabling them to be of service to their own school and community.

Academics

Programs Offered

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS)

Guided by its commitment to academic excellence and innovation, the College continuously enhances its curriculum, learning environment, and industry partnerships to ensure that graduates are well-prepared to become responsible technology professionals, entrepreneurs, researchers, and future leaders in the field of computing.

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT)

The BSIT program prepares students to design, implement, manage, and maintain information systems and technology infrastructures. The curriculum emphasizes software development, networking, database management, web and mobile application development, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and IT project management to meet the demands of modern organizations.

Academics

PROGRAM OUTCOMES

The BSCS and BSIT programs prepare students for entry into the dynamic world of computing and IT professionals, emphasizing technical skills, professional competencies, and ethical values. After completion of the program, graduates are expected to:

  • Perform computing-related tasks effectively and efficiently in accordance with industry standards in the development, management, and maintenance of computer-based systems;
  • Undertake roles and responsibilities in software development, networking, database management, technical support, and system administration in business, government, and private organizations following recognized competency standards;
  • Analyze computing problems, identify technical issues, and implement appropriate solutions through proper system design, programming, troubleshooting, and application of technological tools.
